Salmon by-product valorization

The problem
Norway’s salmon industry uses about 94% of its raw material, yet roughly 36,000 tonnes of salmon blood are discarded every year.
What I am building
Collection, logistics and early-stage processing that turn this stream into a reliable, traceable input for high-value uses such as heme iron, bioactive peptides, pet food and marine fertilizer, together with established processors and research institutions.
Looking for
Processors, feed companies and research institutions.

Synbiotics for nutrition and feed

The problem
Up to 45% of fruit and berry processing volume ends up as by-product, while aquaculture and livestock face growing pressure to reduce antibiotic use.
What I am building
A zero-waste biorefinery model that turns fruit and berry by-products into synbiotic formulations through controlled fermentation. Two markets: capsules and powders for human nutrition, and feed additives for aquaculture and livestock. Built to meet Mattilsynet and EU requirements from day one.
Looking for
Investors, feed producers, researchers and nutraceutical brands.
Contact
synbiotics@pm.me
Since 2025 · onboarding pilot builders

SIP panel construction

The problem
Norwegian builders and cabin developers face rising material costs and slow on-site build times.
What I am building
I bring EU-manufactured structural insulated panels (SIP) to the Norwegian hytte and prefab market, with the documentation builders require: CE marking, Norwegian-language product documentation, and SINTEF technical approval in progress.
Looking for
Pilot builders, reference projects, distribution and OEM supply partners.
